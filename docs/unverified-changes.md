# Changes not yet run in the game

Everything here was written and compiled while testing was on hold. None of it has been run in the game or by any test, and the tests that check it (registered in `tests/run.py`, listed in [tests/README.md](../tests/README.md)) have not been run either: nothing committed after `e0806ab` has run. Results reported in [the build guide](macos-build.md) (the 55-section replay, the walked a10 sections, the device checks) predate all of it. The build guide's Status gives the current state and its "Not yet verified in the game" lists the earlier work by area; the steps for checking it all are in the [test plan](../tests/README.md#test-plan-for-when-testing-resumes). When something here is seen in the game, note the result in the build guide's Status table and take it out of this file.

- **Scenery collision** (`SCENERY_COLLISION`; build guide, Retail collision for other sections, Scenery collision): the scenery batch's Crash Site and c10 sections 3 and 6, the c10 section 5 batch's section 5, the other 50 since the batch after it, d40 section 3 (13 placements) and c10 section 1's `x50_rock` since the d40 section 3 batch, and since the use-aware rule's batch seven named placements a script only reads or changes after their section, baked ten times since b30's three shaft crates stand in two sections (Scripted scenery by use); 4,123 placements, none seen in the game. The walks verified in earlier batches (a10 sections 1 to 4 and 7) predate it, and most routes were re-planned round it. The baked scenery is also stamped into every section's cube raster (the cube-raster stamp), which AssaultCube's flood waypoints follow.
- **Squad sizes and major upgrades** (the squad-count and major-upgrade batch; Difficulty): Halo's squad sizes per difficulty and major upgrades at placement, so fewer actors on Easy and, where a squad's tag `max_count` is below its `min_count`, on Heroic and Legendary, and majors among the placed actors.
- **The difficulty table** (the difficulty-table work; Difficulty): the globals' difficulty table by Halo's team rules replaces the engine's constants, so damage owned by a team hostile to the player is multiplied by enemy_damage whoever it hits (the player, the marines, and the Covenant and the Flood fighting each other) and divided back out on the owner's own side; the player's, the marines' and every actor's body and shield take their team's vitality values, and shields recharge by their team's recharge value.
- **Combat by difficulty** (the combat-scaling work; Difficulty, Combat by difficulty): hostile actors fire faster, more accurately and with shorter pauses on Heroic and Legendary, slower and less often on Easy, throw grenades by the level, and needles and other homing shots turn at half their rate towards the player on Easy and Normal (0.75 Heroic, full Legendary); since the melee-delay and grenade-timeout export an encounter throws one grenade per its tag timeout, and Hunters and sword Elites wait their actr's melee delay after each attack.
- **The Flood tags** (the Flood tag batch; Actor AI, The carrier burst, the infection pop and the speed stagger), in c10, c20, c40, d20 and d40: carriers release 3 to 6 thrown infection forms instead of 4 to 8, an infection form's death pops its neighbours within 0.75 units (one at most on Heroic and Legendary), and c10's nopop forms move faster in combat on Heroic and Legendary and slower on Easy. Since the carrier-squad change the released forms join the carrier's squad, so the carrier spawners of c20 sections 1 to 4, d20 sections 1 to 3 and 5 and d40 sections 1 and 3 to 5 wait for them.
- **Actor shields** (the shield-leak work; Factions and special enemies, Material damage; Difficulty, Shield recharge): a Jackal's shield covers only its hand-held shield, a marine's leaks a fifth or more of every hit to the body and recharges in 1 s after 8 s, and the crewmen, captains, wounded and suicidal marines and stealth Elites have no shield.
- **Damage by material** (the headshot work and the cyborg work; Damage fidelity): a pistol or sniper headshot kills an actor whose shield is down or broken by that shot, the actors' material modifiers are on by default, a modifier of 0 deals full damage, and the player takes each hit through the cyborg's coll, so every time to kill moves.
- **Reverts and Continues** (the stood-members change; Title menu and saved games, Squad members where they stood): living squad members come back where they stood with their health and shield instead of at their starts at full vitality.
- **Cinematics** (the MD3 range fix; Import, build and play), in a10, a30, a50, c10 and d20: 27 clips that used to be left out now play (x30's lifepods, a30's insertion pod, a50's energy lift, c10's x50 Chief and marines, d20's jump down), and saves in those missions restart their section's script on Continue.
- **Staged fights** (the playfight change), in a10, a30, b40, d20 and d40: encounters under `ai_playfight` deal 0.3 of their damage instead of none, whole encounters and later members included.
- **Scripted vitality and blasts** (the script-vitality change and the effect-damage default change): `unit_set_current_vitality` and `unit_set_maximum_vitality` take Halo's points (the crews and marines a10's and c10's scripts set keep a point or less), and the scripts' `effect_new` blasts deal their damage by default.
- **Actor melee** (the actor-melee work; Actor AI, Halo's actor melee), everywhere Elites or the Flood fight and most of all in c10, c20, c40, d20 and d40: armed Elites and Flood combat forms melee at close range between bursts, the unarmed combat forms (322 of c10's 585 combat-form starts) now hurt anyone (20 to 30 or 12 to 16 a blow) and drop no gun, infection forms deal 3 and are spent on a shield (Halo's continuous melee without one) where they swung for 18, carriers burst at their target instead of swinging for 36, and the stealth sword Elite's blow is 151, not 75.
- **Falling damage** (the falling-damage work; Damage fidelity, Falling damage), everywhere: the player and the actors take Halo's falling damage, none under 3 Halo units of drop, up to death at 6 (Halo's own thresholds, applied to the engine's descent rather than its speed); no planned route drop reaches 3 units (`tools/halo_fall_scan.py`), but jumping or walking off a high ledge off the route now hurts or kills, and actors knocked off ledges can die.
- **Script damage by its jpt!** (the script-jpt change; Actor AI), in a10, a30, b30, c20, c40 and d40: c40's three pulse generators empty the player's shield and leave the health (the old flat 40 could kill a low-health player), a10's shield-tutorial zap does nothing, d40's ovens burn 30 a call and a30's emitter shock 30 to 50; the guaranteed kills spare a player under `object_cannot_take_damage`.
- **Overcharged plasma bolts** (the special-fire change; Actor AI, Special fire), wherever Jackals fight: a Jackal now and then holds its plasma pistol through a pause and opens the next burst with the tracking, shield-draining charged bolt at 0.2 of its damage, then overheats; an actor's weapon battery no longer drains.
